This seminar features student presentations on in-progress or published research, as well as invited external academic and industry speakers. Students enrolled in the seminar for credit are required to present at least once, and may choose to either present their own work or a published paper from a related journal or conference (e.g., POPL, PLDI, OOPSLA, ICSE, FSE, CAV).

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Programming Language Design & Semantics
  • Type Systems
  • Program Analysis & Verification
  • Model Checking & Automated Reasoning
  • Proof Assistants & Interactive Theorem Proving
  • Program Synthesis & Repair
  • Software Testing & Debugging
  • Machine Learning for Code

Interested in sharing your work? Send an email to the organizers with the title of your presentation and a link to your paper (if applicable).
